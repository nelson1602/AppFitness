## Not medical advice

AppFitnessRD is a fitness and wellness tool. Its nutrition guidance, workout
plans, progress summaries, and safety-aware movement exclusions are
deterministic informational outputs derived from data you enter and the
versioned rules described in `.ai/07_ICOACH.md`. They are **not medical advice,
diagnosis, treatment, professional dietary advice, or medical clearance**, and
AppFitnessRD is **not a medical device**. Progress trends and weekly snapshots
are descriptive fitness/wellness signals, not clinical assessments.

## Professional physical evaluation

The app recommends that users consider a professional physical evaluation. It
records only whether you report completing one, an optional date, and
self-declared areas or movements to avoid. It does **not** collect the
professional's results, findings, diagnosis, notes, medication information,
treatment instructions, or clearance decision through the public-v1 profile.
Recording completion never means that the app has verified, approved, or
medically cleared you.

## Consult a qualified professional

Seek advice from a qualified physician or other appropriate professional before
starting, changing, or stopping an exercise, nutrition, or health program,
especially if you have a condition, injury, are pregnant, take medication, or
are unsure whether an activity is appropriate. Never disregard or delay
professional advice because of information from this app.

The app does not recommend medications or supplement products/dosages and does
not evaluate medication or supplement interactions.

## Emergencies

The app is not for emergencies. If you think you may have a medical emergency,
contact your local emergency services immediately.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the data you enter and
for how you act on any output. Recommendations may be limited or inappropriate
when inputs are missing or inaccurate.

## Deterministic and safety-aware — not a clinician

The coaching engine may conservatively exclude movements that you explicitly
declare should be avoided. It does not infer a diagnosis, severity, workload
reduction, professional restriction, or clearance from an affected-area choice.
Its deterministic and explainable design does not make it a substitute for
individualized professional judgment.

## Added / Changed / Fixed

### Added (Phases 13–17)
- **Profile & goal entry** (Phase 13) — on-device profile and goal setup.
- **Medical / physical evaluation** (Phase 14) — evaluation entry + history,
  restrictions/injuries management; sensitive free-text encrypted.
- **Nutrition** (Phase 15) — food catalog, meal planning, food logging with
  daily totals; dietary preferences & allergies with non-blocking warnings.
- **Workout** (Phase 16) — routine builder, workout logging (sets/reps/weight),
  exercise library incl. user custom exercises; read-only iCoach TrainingPlan
  integration.
- **Progress monitoring** (Phase 17 / ADR-P016) — body-weight & measurement
  entry, deterministic weekly snapshots (avg weight, training volume, calories,
  workout count, deload flag), in-house trend charts, and a dashboard progress
  card.
- Account deletion surface (`/delete-account`) — immediate, irreversible
  (ADR-P011).

## Security & privacy

- Health free-text (medical notes/conditions/medications, restriction notes,
  dietary-preference notes) is AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest (device + server);
  wellness/fitness data (progress body metrics, workout, food logs) is plaintext
  at rest, protected by app-private storage, TLS, and access controls (ADR-P016
  D1). TLS in transit; passwords Argon2-hashed; tokens hashed.
- Legal drafts (`docs/legal/*`) and the Play Data Safety mapping were refreshed
  to the current feature set (Phase 20 Slice 2) but remain **DRAFT pending legal
  sign-off** — see Known issues.
- Monitoring (Sentry) is wired but **inert** (no DSN); scrubbers exclude
  PII/PHI by design (ADR-P010). Enablement runbook: `docs/SENTRY_ENABLEMENT.md`.
